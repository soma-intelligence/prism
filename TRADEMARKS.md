## What you can do

- Build and run Prism from this source for yourself, under its own name and icons.
- Say that your project is based on Prism by Soma, or that it works with Prism.
- Write about Prism and Soma, and show the app as it is.

## What a fork must change

If you distribute a build of a fork, through the App Store, TestFlight or any other channel, it needs its own identity:

- a name that isn't Soma or Soma Labs and doesn't suggest that Soma Labs made or endorses it. We also ask that you don't call your fork Prism, so people can tell the two apart;
- its own app icons, and no Soma mark (the `AppIcon` and `IconPreview` image sets and the `soma.mark` symbol in `App/Prism/Assets.xcassets`);
- its own bundle id, set in `App/Config/Signing.xcconfig`, and its own app group in place of `group.Soma.Prism`.
